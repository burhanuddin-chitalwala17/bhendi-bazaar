# ADR-0041: An auction winner buys through an order priced from their accepted offer

- **Date:** 2026-09-28
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Context:** Bidding shipped with settlement by hand: the platform phoned the winner, took the money off the platform, and typed what it sold for ([bidding spec](../specs/bidding/spec.md) R38, H7). That held only while there were a handful of auctions. [winner-offer](../specs/bidding/winner-offer/) automates the sale: the winner gets an offer by email, pays through a checkout, and silence passes the item down the ladder. Two standing decisions were in the way. Every order line is priced from `Product.price` through the one effective-price function ([ADR-0002](0002-server-holds-pricing-authority.md), [ADR-0018](0018-one-effective-price-function.md)), and a winning bid is not that price. [ADR-0023](0023-bidding-status-stores-only-what-the-clock-cannot-decide.md) also said nothing about bidding runs on a schedule, while an offer that nobody answers has to pass on without anyone looking.
- **Decision:**
  1. **The winner's purchase is an ordinary `Order`.** Address, shipping, gateway, stock reservation, abandonment, fulfilment and the org ledger are the ones every order uses. Commission follows the organisation's own terms like any other sale. No second sale path exists for auctions.
  2. **Its one item line is priced from the accepted `BidOffer`, read by the server inside the order transaction.** This is the single exception to pricing from the catalogue. The request carries the offer's token and never an amount, so the server still holds pricing authority (Invariant 1). Only the source of the price changes, and only for a line bound to an offer that is accepted, unexpired, and for that product and option. No offer or coupon applies to that line ([ADR-0019](0019-discount-is-one-winning-offer.md)). Shipping is charged on top of it as on any order.
  3. **The purchase gate lets exactly that claim through.** `assertNotUnderBidding` passes the claimed offer's product and refuses every other purchase of it, as before.
  4. **An auction's end may trigger work; the auction's own state still may not depend on it.** The phase remains derived from the clock (ADR-0023 decision 1 stands). The offer chain is moved forward by a job enqueued for `endAt` and an hourly sweep on the worker ([ADR-0038](0038-background-work-runs-on-an-owned-worker-against-a-postgres-job-queue.md)). Whether an offer can still be acted on is derived from its `expiresAt` at the moment of acting, so a late job delays the next email but never lets a late acceptance through.
  5. **Events created before this decision keep manual settlement**, marked by a null claim window, and nothing automated ever touches them.
- **Alternatives considered:**
  - **A standalone gateway payment link, with no order.** This kept "a bidding sale is not an order" and is the smaller change. It was rejected because it automates only half the sale. The address, the shipping and the fulfilment would still be a conversation, and the gateway integration would gain a second payment path with its own signature and amount checks to keep in step with the first ([ADR-0005](0005-payment-state-server-only.md)).
  - **Price the line from the catalogue and apply the gap as a discount.** This fits ADR-0018 on paper. It was rejected because the bid is not a reduction of anything: the gap can be negative, which a discount cannot express, and it would enter the offer engine's funding split and the org's commission base as a promotion nobody ran.
  - **Admin-triggered offers, keeping the auction free of any schedule.** This is closest to ADR-0023. It was rejected on the product decision that an auction ends at its stated time for the bidder too. Waiting for someone to click is the delay this feature exists to remove.
- **Consequences:**
  - ✅ An auction sale ships, pays out and appears to the org exactly like any sale, with no parallel ledger or fulfilment path.
  - ✅ Commission, shipping and abandonment need no auction-specific rules.
  - ⚠️ `createOrderWithShipments` now has a second pricing source. The claim is the only way in, and it is mutation-tested; a second exception would need its own ADR.
  - ⚠️ The organisation now sees the winner as the customer on an order, which the bidding spec's R36 had ruled out. The bid ladder and losing bidders stay hidden.
  - ⚠️ Bidding now has work that runs on the worker. A worker outage delays the next offer by the length of the outage. It never extends anyone's deadline and never sells the item twice.
