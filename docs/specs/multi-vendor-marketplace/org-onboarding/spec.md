## What this feature is
Someone with no organisation can create one and arrive in a working portal.

## Why it is separate
Pages do not exist yet. Basic drafts are acceptable; the flow matters more than the styling.

## Requirements (provisional)
- **R1** — A person with no organisation who opens the selling portal is offered creation rather than an error.
- **R2** — Creating an organisation collects only what the record requires, and the creator becomes its first member.
- **R3** — A first pickup location is collected during creation or immediately after, because an organisation that cannot ship is not usable.
- **R4** — The organisation and its first membership are created together or not at all.
