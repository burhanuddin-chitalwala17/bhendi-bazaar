|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q2 | What is a conversation? | One thread between the shop and one person on one platform account: a DM or chat thread, or one comment thread under one post (the comment and its replies). Keyed by the platform's own thread id, unique per (shop, source, thread id) | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | What every inbox in the field shows as one row; a re-delivered event finds the same thread | implemented (unit 1) |
| Q3 | What is unread, and how is the inbox searched? | A conversation is unread when its newest message is from the person and arrived after any member last opened it; opening the thread marks it read for the whole shop. Each tab has an Unread chip, and search finds a person by name or handle; there is no full-text search of messages | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | Shared inbox (parent Q63): one shop-wide read state, not one per member | decided |
| Q5 | What can a message carry, in and out? | A reply is text up to 1,000 characters and one image from the shop's media library: no voice notes, stickers or files. Arriving voice notes, photos, videos, documents and locations show as what they are, voice notes playing in the thread; anything the inbox cannot show (a view-once photo, a poll, a reel share) appears as a line naming what it was, with its time, never dropped silently. Replaced in place 2026-11-23 (owner, voice notes play); was: voice notes and videos shown only as a line | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | Covers selling conversations; both platforms accept text and an image. Instagram's text cap is 1,000 | decided |
| Q7 | How does the reply box behave when a platform's reply window has shut? | The port tells the tool the window: open (reply freely), human-agent (parent Q36: a reply allowed until day 7, sent tagged), or shut. Shut on WhatsApp offers approved templates with each one's cost (parent Q37); shut on Instagram explains Meta allows no reply until the person writes again. Never disabled | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | ADR-0053; the window rules belong to each platform, so the plug reports them | decided |
| Q9 | What happens when a send fails? | The message shows Not sent with the plain reason and Try again. A temporary failure is retried once automatically first; a dead connection says Reconnect | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | Mirrors parent Q51's pattern for posts | decided |
| Q15 | Does the inbox store the person's phone number or profile photo? | The display name and handle (Instagram) or number (WhatsApp) as the platform gives them; no profile photo, whose links expire. A WhatsApp number is contact data (ADR-0024), never an identity key across shops | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | Hold only what the row needs (DPDP) | implemented (unit 1) |
| Q16 | Can a member take back a message? | Not once sent: neither WhatsApp's nor Instagram's API lets a business edit or delete its own sent message. Instead every message a member sends from a thread (a reply, a template, a checkout link) is held 7 seconds with Undo, which puts it back in the box; the hold is kept on our side, so leaving the thread or closing the tab still sends it at the end. Automatic replies are not held | Owner | 2026-11-23 | The nearest honest thing to edit and delete that the platforms allow | decided |
| Q17 | How does the change feed's cursor get its numbers? | Each table's `changeSeq` has its own sequence (`Conversation_changeSeq_seq`, `Message_changeSeq_seq`), taken afresh on every write, and every message change also bumps its conversation: the list reads the conversation cursor, an open thread its own message cursor. Departs from the TRD's one shared `inbox_change_seq`. Built in unit 1 | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-25 | Prisma's schema diff drops a sequence no column owns, so every later migration generated by any lane would carry a `DROP SEQUENCE`. **For unit 2:** sequence numbers are taken inside transactions that commit out of order (two connections of one shop drain at once), so the feed must not read past the oldest in-flight number (read `changeSeq < ` the lowest uncommitted value, or with a short lag), or a change is skipped | implemented (unit 1) |
| Q18 | What does D1's model add, to land every fact? | `Message.label` (what an unshowable message was, in our words, R8), `Message.refersTo` (the platform id a reaction or edit acts on), `Conversation.lastMessageKind` (so a list row says "Photo" without opening a sealed body); `Message.state` is null for what the person sent; a message carrying several files is one row per file (`#n` on its dedupe key); `memberId` has no foreign key until unit 3 writes it. Who a connection belongs to is asked of channels' new `social-owners.service` | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-25 | Each is a column a sink needs to keep what arrived without dropping it; none changes a requirement. The migration also lays D4's and D8's partial count and name-search indexes now, with the tables. A status that arrives before the row it names (an app echo not yet stored) changes nothing and is not retried: the echo lands as SENT; unit 3's own sends carry their row id as WhatsApp's callback, so this touches only messages sent from the platform's app | implemented (unit 1) |
| Q19 | When does a conversation wait, and what is one waiting spell? | Waiting: Needs you or unread, with the person's newest message at least 4 hours old. `Conversation.waitingEmittedAt` marks the spell told about; a spell ends when the thread stops waiting, and the next inbound message (or a Needs you mark) on a thread nothing waited on clears the mark, so the next spell is told about afresh. The sweep (`inbox.waiting`) is woken for each arrival's due moment with `enqueueLater` (10-minute slots) and re-arms itself for the next due thread, so it uses no schedule slot. Its still-open check, `inboxWaiting.stillWaiting`, is the `conversation waiting` line of notifications' `still-open.registry.ts` (notifications-core Q10). `markNeedsYou` lives on `inboxWaiting`, not a single `inboxService` | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-25 | D5 names the guard but not when a spell ends; ending it where a thread starts waiting again needs no other unit to clear the mark, and a spell restarted between two sweeps is never missed | implemented (unit 4) |
| Q20 | How does retention know a thread's account, and which job runs it? | Each thread keeps `accountRef`, the account it is with (Instagram account, WhatsApp number), from channels' `socialOwners`. `inbox.retention` deletes, in batches of 1,000, every thread of a connection disconnected 90 days, and a past account's threads once the connection has been on another account 90 days (switching is disconnect then connect, so at least 90 days after the disconnect); a thread whose account is unknown is kept. Daily, woken by the waiting sweep, then by itself. A person's deletion deletes the threads they started in every shop, at once (the `inbox` forgetter); a reply they wrote in another person's comment thread is not attributable (a message has no person) and stays until its thread goes. Departs from D10's `social.retention` and the `connectionClosed` command: one job would have to import every domain, and a pull from channels' state needs no call from channels | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-25 | "Not reconnected to the same account" cannot be told without the thread's account; pulling avoids a channels-to-inbox dependency | implemented (unit 4) |
| Q21 | What does a support share allow, and what does the shop see? | A member shares one chat for one of the shop's open requests (`requestService.isOpenForShop`), for 48 hours; Stop sharing ends every live share of it at once. Support's open (`inboxPrivacy.openForSupport`) finds the chat only while a share is live, logs a `SealAccess` row before reading, and returns its newest 200 messages. The shop's log shows when and which chat, never who at support; it outlives a deleted chat. Sharing is Social selling; the log and Stop sharing are free (privacy is never locked). The support-side screen is not in ux.md and stays with parent Q34's audit screen (Later): the service and its guard are built and tested, no admin route calls it yet. The shop-side screens (privacy sheet, Share with support) are unit 5's; `privacyApiClient` is their client | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-25 | R14 and A7 hold by construction at the one door; no ux exists for the support side | implemented (unit 4) |
