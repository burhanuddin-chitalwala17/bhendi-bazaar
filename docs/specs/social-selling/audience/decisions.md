| Q1 | What is a contact, and who counts as one? | One person known to one shop, keyed by (shop, WhatsApp business-scoped id) with the phone number when Meta gives it, or by (shop, phone in E.164) for one that came from an order or an upload, with an optional name and email (parent Q96). The same person at two shops is two contacts; nothing about one is visible to the other. Past buyers are read live from orders and shown as Not yet in the Buyers count, never stored as contacts until they opt in | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-21 | One shop's audience is its own; Meta may withhold the number. was: keyed by (shop, phone in E.164) only | decided |
| Q3 | What does a consent record hold? | Sender (a shop, or the platform: Bhendi Bazaar's own consents sit in the same table, with no platform list or screen until platform sending, parent Q27), channel (WhatsApp or email), state, source, when, and evidence (the order id, the inbound message id or the attestation id). States are Opted in and Stopped; Not yet is the absence of an opt-in, never a stored row. Changes are appended, never overwritten, so the history shows every opt-in and stop | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | DPDP and Meta both ask "when and how did they agree"; a history answers it | decided |
| Q5 | How does audience reach checkout, orders, wishlists and the WhatsApp send without importing them? | Through jobs and public services only. Checkout records the tick on the order and, on payment confirmed, enqueues one shared job, `audience.checkout-opt-in` (order id, shops, phone, email, the box's wording version); checkout never imports audience. Orders and wishlists are read through checkout's and wishlist's public services ("buyers of shop X since a date or in a category", "wishlisters of shop X's products"), returning phones, never addresses. Towards sending, audience answers `consentFor(shop, phone, channel)` and emits `audience.consent-changed`; it never counts sends, the caps staying with the WhatsApp send in campaigns | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | The stock-changed pattern (ADR-0038); "side effects belong to the transition that earns them" (checkout CLAUDE.md) | decided |
| Q8 | What is the checkout box, and when does it record consent? | Unticked by default, reading "Send me offers from <shop names> and Bhendi Bazaar on WhatsApp and email. Stop any time." Guests get it too, their phone and email being on the order. Consent is recorded at payment confirmed, so an abandoned checkout records nothing, and goes to every shop in the order plus the platform, each on WhatsApp and email | Owner | 2026-11-06 | Names both senders and both channels (parent Q27) | decided |
| Q10 | How does the store page's opt-in work? | Shown only on a shop whose WhatsApp is connected, in the storefront's header and footer. "Get offers on WhatsApp" opens `wa.me/<shop number>` with "YES <shop code>" pre-filled; any inbound message to that number whose text, trimmed and case-folded, is YES, HAAN or हाँ (with or without the code) opts the sender in, the message id being the evidence. It opts in to that shop on WhatsApp only: the checkout box is the one place both senders and both channels are named | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | The number already identifies the shop; the code only helps if a shop shares a number later. Hindi words honour parent Q37 | decided |
| Q13 | What stops consent, and can it restart? | STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, बंद or रोको sent alone on WhatsApp to a shop (trimmed, case-folded) stops that shop's WhatsApp consent; the email unsubscribe link stops that sender's email consent; neither touches another sender or channel (parent Q9). A stopped contact may opt in again by any opt-in source, the history keeping both, but an uploaded list can never re-opt-in someone who stopped | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | Exact words only, so "stop sending the wrong size" is a chat, not an opt-out; Hindi per parent Q37 | decided |
| Q15 | What does an upload accept? | CSV or XLSX, up to 5,000 rows: phone (required), name and email (optional). Invalid phones are skipped and counted; duplicates merge into one contact. The attestation tick (parent Q9) is required to make them Opted in; without it they arrive as Not yet | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | Bulk-sheet's file handling exists; 5,000 is above any shop we have, and a bigger file is a split, not a gate | decided |
| Q17 | Which list filters exist (parent Q75)? | Opted in on WhatsApp (always applied), bought from a category, wishlisted any of the shop's products, last order within N days, and member of an uploaded list. Filters combine with AND. A list is a saved filter, counted live, never a stored copy of members | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | A saved filter cannot hold a contact who has since stopped | decided |
| Q19 | Who sees a contact's details? | Members of that shop, on the Audience page, with the phone masked to its last four digits; the full number shows on tap and the view is audit-logged. Contacts cannot be exported | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | The shop needs to recognise people; the mask stops shoulder-surfing and bulk copying | decided |
| Q21 | How long is consent kept, and what does a deletion request remove? | Consent history is kept three years after its last change. A person's deletion request removes their contact at every shop holding it, with its list memberships and consents, leaving only a minimal "stopped" record (sender, channel, hashed phone, date) so a later upload cannot silently re-add them | Owner | 2026-11-06 | Proof if Meta or a buyer disputes consent | decided |
