| Q1 | What is a contact? | One person known to one shop, keyed by (shop, phone in E.164), with an optional name and email. The same phone at two shops is two contacts; nothing about one is visible to the other | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | Consent is given to a shop (parent Q27), and a phone is contact data, not identity (ADR-0024) | decided |
| Q2 | Where are Bhendi Bazaar's own consents kept, since the platform is a sender too (parent Q27)? | In the same consent table, with the sender recorded as the platform instead of a shop. | Q3 | What does a consent record hold? | Sender (shop or platform), channel (WhatsApp or email), state (opted in / stopped), source, when, and evidence: the order id, the inbound message id, or the attestation id. Changes are appended, never overwritten, so the history shows every opt-in and stop | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | DPDP and Meta both ask "when and how did they agree"; a history answers it | decided |
| Q4 | Which consent states exist? | Not yet (no record), Opted in, Stopped. "Not yet" is the absence of an opt-in, never a stored row | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | Three plain words a seller understands; a missing opt-in can never be mistaken for one | decided |
| Q5 | How does the checkout box reach audience without audience importing checkout? | Checkout records the tick on the order; on payment confirmed it enqueues one shared job, `audience.checkout-opt-in`, carrying the order id, shop(s), phone, email and the box's wording version. Audience handles it. Checkout never imports audience | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | The stock-changed pattern (ADR-0038); "side effects belong to the transition that earns them" (checkout CLAUDE.md) | decided |
| Q6 | When is a checkout opt-in recorded: at placement or at payment? | At payment confirmed. An abandoned checkout records nothing | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | An unpaid checkout is shown to nobody (checkout-abandonment); a consent on a ghost order is noise | decided |
| Q7 | An order with parcels from two shops: who gets consent? | Every shop in the order, plus the platform, each on WhatsApp and email | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | The box names "the shops you bought from"; the buyer saw each | decided |
| Q8 | Checkout box wording? | Unticked by default: "Send me offers from <shop names> and Bhendi Bazaar on WhatsApp and email. Stop any time." | Owner | 2026-11-06 | Names both senders and both channels (parent Q27) | decided |
| Q9 | Does a guest checkout get the box? | Yes: the guest's phone and email are on the order; the consent is the same | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | Guests are buyers too; consent needs no account | decided |
| Q10 | How is a store-page YES matched to a shop (parent Q59)? | The "Get offers on WhatsApp" link opens `wa.me/<shop number>` with the text "YES <shop code>" pre-filled. The inbound message id is the evidence | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | The number already identifies the shop; the code only helps if a shop shares a number later. Hindi words honour parent Q65 | decided |
| Q11 | Where does the store-page button show? | Only on a shop whose WhatsApp is connected: the shop's storefront header and footer. A shop without WhatsApp shows nothing | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | A link to an unconnected number captures nothing | decided |
| Q12 | Does a store-page opt-in also give the platform consent? | No. It opts in to that shop on WhatsApp only. The checkout box is the one place both senders and both channels are named | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | The buyer messaged the shop, not us; consent covers only what was named | decided |
| Q13 | What does STOP match? | STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, बंद or रोको, alone, trimmed and case-folded, on WhatsApp to that shop: stops that shop's WhatsApp consent. The email unsubscribe link stops that sender's email consent. Neither touches another sender or channel (parent Q62) | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | Exact words only, so "stop sending the wrong size" is a chat, not an opt-out; Hindi per parent Q65 | decided |
| Q14 | Can a stopped contact opt in again? | Yes, by any opt-in source; the history keeps both. An uploaded list can never re-opt-in a contact who stopped | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | A person may change their mind; a shop's file may not change it for them | decided |
| Q15 | What does an upload accept? | CSV or XLSX, up to 5,000 rows: phone (required), name and email (optional). Invalid phones are skipped and counted; duplicates merge into one contact. The attestation tick (parent Q45) is required to make them Opted in; without it they arrive as Not yet | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | Bulk-sheet's file handling exists; 5,000 is above any shop we have, and a bigger file is a split, not a gate | decided |
| Q16 | Is the Hindi preference per shop or global? | Per shop: "HINDI" to a shop switches that shop's messages only (parent Q65) | Owner | 2026-11-06 | A phone is not an identity across shops (ADR-0024) | decided |
| Q17 | Which list filters exist (parent Q75)? | Opted in on WhatsApp (always applied), bought from a category, wishlisted any of the shop's products, last order within N days, and member of an uploaded list. Filters combine with AND. A list is a saved filter, counted live, never a stored copy of members | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | A saved filter cannot hold a contact who has since stopped | decided |
| Q18 | How does audience read orders and wishlists without importing their repositories? | Through checkout's and wishlist's public services: a "buyers of shop X since date / in category" read and a "wishlisters of shop X's products" read, each returning phones, never addresses | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | ADR-0033; checkout trims buyers to name, city, pincode for shops, and phones only leave for opted-in matching | decided |
| Q19 | Who sees a contact's phone? | Members of that shop, on the Audience page, masked to the last four digits; the full number is shown on tap and the view is audit-logged | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | The shop needs to recognise people; the mask stops shoulder-surfing and bulk copying | decided |
| Q20 | Can a shop export its contacts? | No, not in this subfeature | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | Export is a new capability no acceptance needs; it would also carry consent off-platform | parked |
| Q21 | How long is consent history kept? | Three years after its last change, and deleted on a person's deletion request | Owner | 2026-11-06 | Proof if Meta or a buyer disputes consent | decided |
| Q22 | What does a person's deletion request remove? | Their contact at every shop that holds it, its list memberships and its consents, except a minimal "stopped" record (sender, channel, hashed phone, date) so a later upload cannot silently re-add them | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | Deletion without a suppression marker would let the next upload undo it | decided |
| Q23 | How does the WhatsApp channel ask "may I send?" (parent Q68)? | Audience answers `consentFor(shop, phone, channel)` and emits `audience.consent-changed`; the caps stay with the WhatsApp send in campaigns. Audience never counts sends | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | Consent is audience's fact; frequency and spend are the send's (parent Q68) | decided |
| Q24 | Do past buyers appear as contacts (parent Q58)? | Yes, read live from orders as Not yet in the Audience page's "Buyers" count, never stored as contacts until they opt in | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | The shop sees its reach; nothing personal is copied that has no consent behind it | decided |
