| Q17 | How does the built connect differ from TRD D1's wording? | Connect is a `GET` link answered with a redirect, like the marketplaces' (a browser follows it to Instagram), not a `POST`. The callback is exempt from rate limiting, as the marketplaces' is. The webhook field is `message_reactions` (D1 said `messaging_reactions`). Connects for one shop are serialised with an advisory lock, since a first connect has no row to lock. The marketplaces' daily pass and cross-shop reads skip social rows; the Instagram pass is unit 2's | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-24 | The fresh-context review of unit 1 found the drift and the race | implemented (unit 1) |
| Q18 | How does the built renewal, health and disconnect differ from TRD D2, D4, D5's wording? | A refused renewal or account read is the existing `revoked` code (D2 said `TOKEN_REVOKED`). Needs reconnecting reaches Home through the channels overview's attention, as the marketplaces' does, and for a shop with Social selling even without Channels; the `connection needs you` event waits for notifications-core, which is not built. Check now answers from a check under a minute old instead of a rate-limit policy: its route is portal-guarded, so no public policy applies; two taps in the same instant may each ask Instagram once, which a member's own taps cannot multiply further. Inbox's `connectionClosed` has no caller yet: inbox-core is not built, and reads the record's `disconnectedAt` when it is | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-24 | Building unit 2 against what exists: notifications-core and inbox-core are later in phase 2 | implemented (unit 2) |
| Q19 | How does the built card differ from TRD D3, D4 and ux.md? | The checklist is computed from facts on the connection: `repliesAskedAt` (the sheet's Done, through `POST …/instagram/replies`, free since it calls nothing at Instagram), `firstMessageAt` (channels' `channels.message-access` sink on any inbound message), `messagesOffAt` (`instagramConnection.messagesRefused`, for inbox-instagram to call; cleared by a newer message). The @username and Business or Creator are stored at connect and refreshed by each check. First post and Comment-to-DM read as not done and open nothing until post-composer and comment-automations ship, so a card whose next step is one of them has no main button, and the connected note has no saved-posts line.
