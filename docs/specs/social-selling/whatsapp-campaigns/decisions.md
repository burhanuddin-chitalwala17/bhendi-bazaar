| Q8 | Is the send port's caller trusted on consent? | No. The port takes a recipient, a template and its values; it checks consent, stop, and both caps itself and answers sent, skipped (with the reason) or held. | Q12 | When does a scheduled campaign go? | At the minute the shop picked, IST, any time of day (parent Q55: the shop decides). | Q17 | What language does a buyer get? | Their stored choice (parent Q65), English by default; a campaign picks a template that exists in both, and a buyer whose language version is not approved gets English | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | Never skip a buyer for a missing translation | decided |
| Q18 | How fast does a campaign send? | Within Meta's throughput (20 per second under coexistence) and the shop's messaging tier; queued in batches on the worker | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-06 | INTEGRATIONS.md; Meta enforces its own tiers | decided |
