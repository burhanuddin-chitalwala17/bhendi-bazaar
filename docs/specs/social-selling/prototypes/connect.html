Disconnect

WhatsApp dialog: what stops, what stays
WWhatsApp● Connected
Disconnect WhatsApp?
Stops: your catalogue stops updating, chats stop arriving here, and messages can't be sent.
Stays: your number keeps working in the WhatsApp Business app. Your chats stay here for 90 days, then are deleted.
To remove us completely, also remove Bhendi Bazaar in Meta Business Suite → Settings → Integrations.
·
Instagram dialog
Disconnect Instagram?
Stops: scheduled posts won't publish, and new comments and DMs stop arriving here.
Stays: your posts on Instagram stay as they are. Your conversations stay here for 90 days, then are deleted.
To remove us completely, also remove Bhendi Bazaar in Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites.
