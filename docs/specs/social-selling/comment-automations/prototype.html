3 · When it goes wrong

R5–R7, R10 · Q4, Q6

Each outcome a shop can see on an activity row, with the reason shown on tap.

Already sent

"This person already got your DM for this post."

Too late

"Instagram only lets shops reply by DM within 7 days of a comment."

Not sent · connection

"Instagram needs reconnecting. DMs resume once it's connected."
Reconnect

Not sent · no link

"The product in this DM was archived, so there was no link to send. Edit the post's DM to send something else."
Edit DM

Waiting

"Lots of comments at once. We're sending them as fast as Instagram allows."

Failing banner (5 failures in an hour)

On Home, via notifications: "Comment DMs aren't going out on 1 post." → Open post

5 · Catalogue states and problems

R4, R8, R9 · whatsapp-catalogue Q4, Q8, Q10

Sync now tapped

Button shows "Syncing…", then toast "WhatsApp catalogue is up to date."

3 not accepted by WhatsApp

Panel lists each: "Zari clutch · WhatsApp says: Image too small (min 500×500)." → Fix product

Retryable

"Couldn't reach WhatsApp. We'll keep trying; your catalogue shows its last update." Action: Try again

Stale (no update 24h+)

"Last updated yesterday, 9:14 pm." Action: Sync now

Needs reconnecting

"Catalogue updates are paused until WhatsApp is reconnected." Action: Reconnect

Disconnect dialog line

"Your WhatsApp catalogue stays in your Meta account but stops updating. Delete it in Commerce Manager if you no longer want it."
