## Privacy — additions

**Added to "We collect only what it takes…"**
- If you agree to hear from a shop or from us on WhatsApp or by email: your phone number or email,
  when and where you agreed, and the language you chose.
- If you message a shop on Instagram or WhatsApp, or comment on its posts: those messages and
  comments, your Instagram or WhatsApp name, and when they were sent, so the shop can answer you
  from Bhendi Bazaar.

**Added to "We share data only with…"**
- Meta (Instagram and WhatsApp), to deliver the messages and posts a shop sends, and to receive the
  messages you send it.

**For shops that connect Instagram or WhatsApp** (new paragraph)
> When a shop connects its Instagram or WhatsApp to Bhendi Bazaar, it lets us act for that account
> only to do what the shop asks: publish its posts, show and answer its messages and comments, keep
> its WhatsApp catalogue up to date, and send messages to people who agreed to receive them. We keep
> the access Meta gives us encrypted, never show it to anyone, and delete it when the shop
> disconnects. We never post or message as ourselves from a shop's account.

**Your choices** (new paragraph)
> One box at checkout lets you hear about offers from the shop you bought from and from Bhendi
> Bazaar, on WhatsApp and by email. We record each separately, so you can stop any one of them on
> its own: reply STOP to that sender on WhatsApp, or use the unsubscribe link in any email. Stopping
> takes effect at once. Order updates are not marketing; they reach you while you have an order.

**How long we keep messages** (new paragraph)
> A shop's conversations with you are kept while that shop is connected to Instagram or WhatsApp
> here, and deleted 90 days after it disconnects. You can ask for yours to be deleted at any time.

**Who can read your messages** (new paragraph)
> Your messages with a shop are read by that shop. Bhendi Bazaar staff do not read them, except to
> look into a complaint, a dispute or a legal request; then a named staff member opens only the
> conversation concerned, records the reason, the shop is told, and every view is logged.

## Terms — additions
> **Connecting Instagram or WhatsApp.** A shop that connects an account confirms it is entitled to
> use it, and stays responsible for what it posts and sends from Bhendi Bazaar, as if sent from the
> app itself. Meta's own terms continue to apply to that account.
>
> **Messages cost the shop.** WhatsApp charges for some messages. Those charges are billed by Meta
> to the shop's own WhatsApp account; Bhendi Bazaar shows the estimated cost before a send and does
> not bill for messages.
>
> **Only people who agreed.** A shop may send marketing messages only to people who agreed to
> receive them. A shop uploading a list confirms each person on it agreed to hear from that shop on
> WhatsApp; that confirmation is recorded. We may stop a shop's sending if Meta or the people it
> messages report misuse.

## Data deletion — a page of its own
Meta requires a public page explaining how to delete data, and a link it can show a person.

> **Deleting your Instagram or WhatsApp data from Bhendi Bazaar**
>
> We act on a deletion request at once and complete it within 30 days (Q82).
>
> *If you are a shop:* disconnect Instagram or WhatsApp from Channels in your shop portal. The access
> Meta gave us is deleted at once; your conversations are deleted 90 days later. You can also remove
> Bhendi Bazaar from your Instagram settings (Settings → Apps and websites) or your Meta Business
> settings; we will treat that as a disconnect.
>
> *If you messaged or commented on a shop:* write to us using the contact details below, giving the
> Instagram username or WhatsApp number. We delete your messages, comments and consent records
> within 30 days and confirm by email.
>
> *If you asked through Meta:* when you remove Bhendi Bazaar in Instagram or Facebook and ask for
> your data to be deleted, Meta tells us; we delete it within 30 days and give you a confirmation code. You can
> check its status at the link Meta shows you.
