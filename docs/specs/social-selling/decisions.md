| Q5 | What is the base feature, and what is it made of? | Social selling: a shop sells through its own Instagram and WhatsApp without leaving Bhendi Bazaar. It connects its accounts once; its products appear in its WhatsApp catalogue, always matching the store; it publishes posts and stories; it answers every message and comment from one inbox, with automatic replies so no enquiry is lost; and it messages customers who asked to hear from it. For shop owners and members, who run it, and buyers, who meet it on Instagram and WhatsApp. Five modules: Connect, Catalogue, Post, Converse (inbox and automatic replies), Reach out (consent and messages to opted-in customers). Siblings it uses, outside it: post generation, notification-hub, bulk-publish, checkout links. Not now: ads, Facebook Page and Threads, paying inside WhatsApp; AI replies come last. | Q7 | How are the modules kept apart? | Only `channels` knows Meta (ADR-0068). The tools are platform-free domains, `server/inbox/`, `server/social/`, `server/audience/`, `server/campaigns/` and `server/automations/`, each declaring a port that `channels` implements per platform; notification-hub extends `server/notifications/`. Meta's events enter at one signed webhook route per Meta product in `channels`, are written once by Meta's id and reach the tools as neutral jobs; each platform inbox also reconciles from the platform's own record on a schedule, since Meta misses webhooks. The boundary test forbids a tool importing a provider | Owner | 2026-11-21 | "One inbox is a tool and we keep adding channels to it" | decided |
| Q8 | Who pays for social selling and its WhatsApp messages, and how is spend controlled? | Social selling is a paid feature with its own lock, a fourth beside Channels, Storefront and Image enhancement (subscriptions/feature-access Q36). Connecting WhatsApp asks for a monthly spending limit over every paid send (campaigns, alerts, inbox templates): an amount or "No limit", never skipped (asked again before the first paid send if left). Each automated alert type is off until turned on, with its estimated cost; at the limit sends stop and the shop is told. Any member may send; cost is shown and confirmed before every paid send, and every send is recorded in paise with who made it and where it came from | Owner | 2026-11-22 | Owner's choice of a lock of its own, so it can be sold and switched separately from marketplaces. was: free at launch, no billing code | decided |
| Q9 | When may a buyer be sent marketing, and how is consent given and withdrawn? | Only once opted in, per sender and channel, with when, where and evidence recorded. Opt-in is the checkout box (Q27), "Get offers on WhatsApp" on the shop page (it opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled YES; sending it is the proof), YES in a chat, or an uploaded list the shop attests to ("These people agreed to get WhatsApp messages from <shop>", recorded with who, when and which file). Contacts synced from the Business app, and buyers from before this feature, start as "Not yet" and are never chased. STOP withdraws that sender's consent at once, with one free confirming reply; every marketing message carries a way to stop | Owner | 2026-11-06 | Meta's policy and India's DPDP Act; a reported number loses its templates | decided |
| Q12 | How does a shop connect WhatsApp? | By Meta's signup pop-up, pre-filled from the shop's profile, which creates a business account inside the flow when there is none. Coexistence first, so the shop keeps its Business app and messages appear in both; verified on day one on the owner's number. A number that cannot use coexistence (blue tick, or Meta refuses) may move fully to Bhendi Bazaar, the portal inbox being its WhatsApp. The connect screen says, as a subtle line, that the catalogue is then managed from Bhendi Bazaar while chats, broadcasts and Status stay in the app. The Business app's contacts are synced at once (Meta allows one sync, within 24 hours); chat history is not imported. Re-registering the app offboards the number, shown as Needs reconnecting. Unofficial linked-session tools are never used | Owner | 2026-11-21 | Shops will not give up the app; a missed sync window loses the contacts for good; less personal data held | decided |
| Q14 | Under what identity does Bhendi Bazaar face Meta and buyers? | The owner verifies with Meta as a sole proprietorship, Udyam preferred in the business name (GST in a personal name often fails Meta's checks), using the GST's legal name. The owner starts verification and creates the Meta app, with privacy and data-deletion pages, ahead of the build | Owner | 2026-11-06 | Verification gates the review and takes days; the names must line up for Meta's check | decided |

Re-checked if Meta's rules change | Owner | 2026-11-06 | Promise only what works | decided |
| Q21 | What is Later? | Ads (boosting, Click-to-WhatsApp, catalogue ads), behind their own review and a linked Facebook business account; Facebook Page and Threads posting, each a plug in post-scheduling; checkout inside WhatsApp, with an ADR showing Invariant 2 holds; forms in chat (WhatsApp Flows); Instagram insights | Owner | 2026-11-06 | Each needs its own review, ADR or demand first | parked |

Neither imports the other | Owner | 2026-11-21 | Posting must not wait on, or know, how an image was made | decided |
| Q27 | May Bhendi Bazaar itself message a buyer? | The checkout box names both the shop and Bhendi Bazaar, on WhatsApp and email; four consents are stored (sender × channel), each withdrawn alone (STOP on WhatsApp, the unsubscribe link in email). The platform's consent is recorded now; sending from a platform number is Later | Owner | 2026-11-06 | One box, honest about every sender and channel | decided |
| Q34 | How is people's data kept, read and deleted? | Inbox messages are kept while the channel is connected and deleted 90 days after a disconnect. A person's deletion request is honoured within 30 days, as the data-deletion page states (the system deletes at once; the promise leaves room for backups). Platform staff never read a shop's conversations freely: an audit opens one conversation with a written reason, the shop is told, and every view is logged; that audit screen is Later, and until it exists no admin reads anything. Claude drafts the policy additions from these answers and the owner approves them before they go live (not legal advice) | Owner | 2026-11-06 | Meta's review asks for it, DPDP requires it, and shops must trust us with their customers | decided |
| Q36 | What does the Instagram inbox handle? | DMs, story replies and story mentions (which arrive as DMs), and comments on the shop's posts and reels. A DM is answered within 24 hours, or by a person up to 7 days with Meta's human-agent tag; a comment publicly, or by one private DM within 7 days. Comments can be hidden from here, never deleted | Owner | 2026-11-21 | Story replies are where buyers ask "price?"; delete stays in Instagram | decided |
| Q37 | Which WhatsApp templates exist, and in which languages? | A starter set we submit to each shop's account on connect (new arrivals, offer, back in stock, order update, abandoned cart, auction), plus the shop's own, each shown with Meta's approval status. Past 24 hours the inbox's reply box offers the approved templates with each one's cost, never disabled | Owner | 2026-11-06 | Shops cannot write Meta-approvable templates unaided | decided |
| Q40 | How often, and when, may a buyer get marketing from a shop? | One rule per buyer per shop across WhatsApp and email together: at most once a day and four times a week by default, changeable under Advanced (Q112), with the risk to the shop's number explained. Order updates are exempt | Owner | 2026-11-22 | One limit a shop can understand; a message goes by WhatsApp or else email, never both, so one count is enough. was: a WhatsApp limit (1 a day, 4 a week) and a separate cross-channel limit (3 a day), the stricter winning, and a separate alert window | decided |
| Q47 | What is a shop's WhatsApp catalogue? | A replica of the shop's live products on Bhendi Bazaar: at the price the product page shows (offers included, from the one price resolver), out of stock shown as such, removed when archived, no per-product toggle, the cart on (Q97). A catalogue the shop already has is read and settled on connect, on the same Settle flow as Amazon and Flipkart: each item matched and linked, added as a new product, or left off (a left-off item is removed; the count is shown before confirming). Bhendi Bazaar's details then win, and an item keeps its WhatsApp id so links in old chats open. Reading a Business-app catalogue is verified on day one; if it cannot be read, the import step says so and offers the bulk upload sheet | Owner | 2026-11-21 | One source of truth; the shop's existing work and chat links are kept | decided |
| Q51 | What does posting offer at launch? | Image, carousel, story and reel (a reel's video from the media library). One post picks any of Instagram feed, Instagram story and WhatsApp Status and is scheduled once. Each destination shows Scheduled, Published (with a link) or Failed; a failure says why and offers Publish now, a dead connection Reconnect | Owner | 2026-11-06 | Everything Instagram's API allows | decided |
| Q54 | Which automated alerts reach buyers, and whom? | Opted-in buyers only, inside the caps (Q40). Abandoned cart: once, an hour after it was left, to a signed-in buyer who has not ordered since. Price drop: on any fall in the effective price, to those who wishlisted it. Back in stock: to those who wishlisted it. Auction: outbid, and ending in an hour, to those who bid (the audience a rule, not code). A "Notify me" button is its own vertical | Owner | 2026-11-06 | Demand the shop already has, messaged sparingly | decided |
| Q61 | How do order updates reach buyers? | Order confirmed, shipped and delivered become notification-hub events. Email stays the default; WhatsApp is off until the shop turns it on knowing the cost, then reaches opted-in buyers with the order link and tracking, email following if it is not delivered within 15 minutes. The confirmation email always goes, as the receipt. Cancellations and refunds stay on email | Owner | 2026-11-06 | Buyers expect order news on WhatsApp; the receipt must never depend on it | decided |
| Q63 | What is the inbox? | One shared inbox: every member sees every conversation, and each reply shows which member sent it; assigning is Later. A tab per connected platform, each with an Unread filter. inbox-core is the shell alone, tested against a fake source; each platform's inbox is its own module with its own rules, plugged into its tab | Owner | 2026-11-21 | Small shops; Instagram and WhatsApp differ too much to share more than the shell | decided |
| Q66 | How are shops and buyers told things? | Through `notification-hub`, a global module built beside social-selling and decoupled from it. Social-selling only emits neutral events ("conversation waiting", "post failed", "post due", "connection needs you") and supplies a "deliver by WhatsApp" channel. The hub starts with email, WhatsApp and the unread badge (in-app bell and push later); a shop gets the badge and a daily email of conversations unanswered over 4 hours. Each buyer alert or campaign reaches a buyer once: by WhatsApp when it can, otherwise by email when they opted in to email, every marketing email carrying an unsubscribe link | Owner | 2026-11-06 | One place decides who hears what, and how | decided |
| Q75 | Which lists can a campaign go to? | Opted-in contacts, narrowed by bought from a category, wishlisted, bought in the last N days, or an uploaded list; saved as named lists. Only opted-in members are ever counted or sent to | Owner | 2026-11-06 | Useful segments without reaching anyone who did not ask | decided |
| Q89 | How are enquiries answered when nobody is at the shop? | One auto-replies module in `automations`, platform-free, reacting to the inbox's events and replying through the inbox: comment-to-DM first (a keyword comment on a post gets the shop's DM, the product link suggested), then chat-autoreplies (greeting and away message by business hours, FAQ answers from the shop's settings and policies, product answers read live from price and stock, buttons, handover to a person, a missed-enquiries count). Keyword rules only until step 8: AI wording comes last, and only over the same live data | Owner | 2026-11-21 | No enquiry lost to a busy owner; a wrong price said by an AI is the risk to avoid | decided |
| Q96 | How is a WhatsApp buyer identified? | By Meta's business-scoped user id, with the phone number when Meta gives it; Meta's `user_changed_user_id` event updates it. | Q97 | What happens when a buyer sends a cart from the WhatsApp catalogue? | It arrives in the WhatsApp inbox as an order card, each item at our live price (never the cart's), with one action that sends the buyer a checkout link holding those items; payment and stock move only at our checkout | Owner | 2026-11-21 | A buyer ready to pay; Invariants 1 and 6 keep price and stock ours | decided |
| Q102 | How does a shop's whole range reach a channel? | In one bulk push with the fixes done in bulk first: what is ready and what needs a fix, grouped by problem (no photo, photo too small, no description, a missing fact), fixed for many products at once on the readiness-fix pages, then everything pushed. Built once and channel-neutral (`bulk-publish`): WhatsApp, Amazon and Flipkart in the same build (marketplace-integration Q44) | Owner | 2026-11-21 | One step ahead of the marketplaces' own tools; marketplace-publish sends one product or family at a time | decided |
| Q107 | In what order is it built? | Eight steps across the modules (spec.md's build order): (1) post-generation, side by side with (2) meta-foundation, both connects, whatsapp-catalogue-import, whatsapp-catalogue, notifications-core and bulk-publish; (3) post-composer, instagram-publishing, whatsapp-status-kit; (4) inbox-core; (5) inbox-instagram, consent-core, whatsapp-sending, checkout-links, inbox-whatsapp; (6) comment-automations, enquiry-autoreplies, product-answers, then the single review; (7) audience, whatsapp-campaigns, hub-marketing, order-updates, demand-alerts; (8) ai-autoreplies. | Q108 | Which subfeatures make up each module? | The tree in spec.md, 19 in the base plus ai-autoreplies parked. Five changes from the 2026-11-06 cut, each fixing something built before what it needs or two modules tied together: shop alerts (notifications-core, from hub-core) are built in step 2 so every module tells the shop through one place; consent-core is cut from audience (taking whatsapp-contacts) and built in step 5; whatsapp-sending takes the send port, spend cap and templates (whatsapp-templates) and is built in step 5; checkout-links (a link opening checkout with given items, prices recomputed) is a new sibling in `cart`; bulk-publish leaves social selling as a sibling. Also: meta-foundation holds the Meta plumbing both connections share; comment rules belong to comment-automations, shown as a panel in post-composer (renamed from post-scheduling); chat auto-replies are two subfeatures, enquiry-autoreplies and product-answers; sales per post or message is Later | Owner | 2026-11-21 | An audit of every subfeature against the base feature: five dependencies ran backwards and posting stored automation rules | decided |

| Q111 | How is a shop guided through setting up a connection? | A checklist on each channel's card, each step ticked as it is done and opening its own screen, which returns to the card: for WhatsApp, Connect, Spending limit, Catalogue (items to settle or fix), Templates (in review or approved), Auto-replies; for Instagram, Connect, Allow messages, First post, Comment-to-DM. Everything else (greeting delay, bot pause, per-product alert limits, how often a buyer may be messaged) sits under Advanced, each with its default | Owner | 2026-11-22 | A small shop sets two things; the rest works from defaults | decided |

The shop: one card on its Home (messages needing it, enquiries answered automatically, posts this week, this month's WhatsApp spend against its limit, setup steps left), each figure opening its screen.
