Posts and Status Kit

Prototype for three social-selling subfeatures: post-scheduling, instagram-publishing and whatsapp-status-kit. Phone width (~360px) first. Every string is the one that ships. Buttons are never disabled: an action that can't happen says why when tapped. Draft, for the owner's sign-off.
