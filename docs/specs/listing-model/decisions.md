# Decisions — listing-model

- **Verified:** 2026-11-07

> Replace a Q in place until it is implemented (end its why with "was: …"); supersede only implemented ones ([ADR-0062](../../adr/0062-a-decision-is-replaced-until-it-is-implemented-and-superseded-after.md)).
> Owner decides money, policy, irreversible data, external commitments, taste.
> Claude decides the rest, marked `Claude · reversible`. Other documents cite Q ids.
> Evidence: the audit of 2026-11-06 (how listings work today, verified in code) and Amazon's per-type rules read out of its template; both summarised in [plan.html](plan.html).

| Q | Question | Answer | Decided by | Date | Why | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | What is this programme's one outcome? | A Bhendi Bazaar product holds every attribute any channel we support needs, and each channel's listing is a mapped view of it that says, before sending, exactly what is filled, from where, and what is missing | Owner | 2026-11-06 | Owner: "there must be a product on bb which has all the attributes required to be published on amazon"; today's setup is "an entirely black box" | decided |
| Q2 | Should BB and each channel have their own product types? | Yes. A BB type says what we ask a shop; a channel type (Amazon TOTE_BAG) says what that channel accepts. They are separate models joined by a type map | Owner | 2026-11-06 | Owner: "a product type of bb defines a different thing and the product type of amazon might define something entirely else" | decided |
| Q3 | Does the form ask only connected channels' attributes, or every channel's? | Every supported channel's, from day one. Required: what BB and the shop's connected channels need. Optional: what other channels would need, shown in a collapsed "Also helps on…" group, never blocking | Owner | 2026-11-07 | Owner: connecting a channel later would otherwise leave every listed product with gaps; "we keep the listing broad from day one" | decided |
| Q4 | When is a channel's listing created for a product? | Only once that channel is connected. Connecting shows what is missing across the catalogue ("38 products need Strap type for Flipkart") with a bulk-fill page | Owner | 2026-11-07 | Owner: "the channel copy of that product doesnt get created until the channel is connected" | decided |
| Q5 | Who adds a new attribute (question)? | A developer, by migration. Attributes are database rows so products and channel fields can point at them, but the admin panel never creates one. An admin chooses which attributes a type asks, maps channel fields, and edits allowed answers | Owner | 2026-11-07 | Owner: "lets keep it out of the hands of an admin and we raise new attributes for new development". Keeps ADR-0052's intent (questions are a developer's job) and setup-requests Q32 | decided |
| Q6 | How are a product's values stored? | One row per product per attribute, pointing at the attribute; a channel listing stores only its overrides, also per attribute. No attribute exists twice | Owner | 2026-11-07 | Owner: "have attribute as a fk in our listing models … the source of truth as one … no duplication" | decided |
| Q7 | Is a channel listing a stored copy of the product? | No. It holds the product, its channel type, its overrides, what was last sent and the channel's answer. The payload is built from the product and the field maps whenever it is opened, checked or sent | Owner | 2026-11-07 | Owner asked what "built when needed" means; agreed once explained. A stored copy drifts the moment the product changes | decided |
| Q8 | Are country of origin, manufacturer, packer and importer product attributes? | Yes. The brand fills them by default; a product can override any of them. Channel fields map to the attributes, never to the brand directly | Owner | 2026-11-07 | Owner: "the brand is not filling any required attribute … or even if it is its not an attribute of the product". Today they reach Amazon by a side door the form and readiness cannot see | decided |
| Q9 | Does a product type follow its category? | Yes. A product reads its type from its category, never a stale copy; when a category's type changes, its products are asked only what is new | Owner | 2026-11-07 | Owner: "yes". Today the type is copied at save and drifts | decided |
| Q10 | Which brand does a product without one use, and where does "list as Generic" live? | The shop's default brand. "Registered on Amazon, or list as Generic" moves from the shop setting (setup-requests Q35) onto each brand | Owner | 2026-11-07 | Owner: "yes". One shop can sell a registered brand and unbranded goods; "Your default brand" is false today | decided |
| Q11 | Where does a channel's catalogue of types and fields come from? | Amazon: its Product Type Definitions API, read through the home brand, refreshed daily. Flipkart and Meesho: their templates, already extracted in `listing-node-templates/extracted/`, loaded by migration | Owner | 2026-11-07 | Owner: "yes". Typing a channel's requirements by hand is how they went wrong | decided |
| Q12 | Is there a link between a brand and a category? | No. A brand is who makes it; a category is where buyers find it | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-07 | Owner asked how they link; nothing a channel asks depends on it | decided |
| Q13 | Quick fix for the Tote bag first? | No. Hold the bag listing and solve it end to end | Owner | 2026-11-07 | Owner: "we can hold bag listing for now lets solve this problem e2e" | decided |
| Q14 | How is the programme cut? | Five subfeatures, each shippable alone, in order: channel-catalogue, field-maps, attributes, dynamic-form, retire-old-paths | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-07 | One outcome each (ADR-0061); every phase keeps every channel working | decided |
| Q15 | What does the product-type ↔ category ↔ brand ↔ shop flow look like? | As drawn in plan.html §5: category points at a BB type; product belongs to a category, a brand (default: the shop's) and optionally a family; values inherit own → family → brand → shop default per attribute, the source shown beside each | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-07 | Owner asked for a sophisticated flow diagram; plan.html is the record | decided |
| Q16 | How are product values stored? | Typed rows, one per product per attribute, real foreign keys, each with its source; a JSON projection on the product rebuilt in the same transaction for every read (TRD D2, D3) | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-07 | Owner asked for a reliable, scalable backend and handed the technical side to Claude. Rows keep integrity (Q6); the projection keeps reads one row. Pure EAV reads (Magento) and JSON-only truth (Akeneo) both rejected, ADR-0070 | decided |
| Q17 | Is completeness computed on page load? | No. Stored per product per connected channel, refreshed by a job when the product, a map or a catalogue changes (TRD D9) | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-07 | Today the products list recomputes readiness every view; it does not scale | decided |
| Q18 | Is an Amazon payload checked before sending? | Yes, against Amazon's own JSON Schema for the type, stored in the catalogue; a failure is a gap, never a request (TRD D8). Adds `ajv` | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-07 | Refusals caught by us, not by Amazon | decided |
| Q19 | How is the migration from today's model done? | Expand, backfill in batches with counts verified, switch reads, contract last; each step reversible until contract (TRD D10) | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-07 | No product's answer may be lost (spec R1) | decided |
| Q20 | How are measures stored? | In one canonical unit per family (grams, millimetres, millilitres), with the unit the shop typed kept for display; converted to each channel's unit by its field map | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-07 | Amazon, Flipkart and Meesho each want different units; one conversion point | decided |
| Q21 | Supersede ADR-0051 and ADR-0052? | Yes, by ADR-0070 once accepted | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-07 | Parent Q5, Q6 need attributes as rows; ADR-0052's intent (questions are a developer's job) is kept | decided |
| Q22 | Is the shop told when a channel changes its rules? | Yes: Home shows "Amazon now asks 2 more details for Tote bags" with a link to fill them; readiness updates too | Owner | 2026-11-07 | Owner's answer | decided |
| Q23 | Who makes the mappings? | Claude makes every default mapping, for all five channels, before release: each field's meaning checked against the channel's own documentation (Amazon's attribute definitions and Product Type Definitions schema, Google's product data specification, Meta's catalogue field reference, Flipkart's and Meesho's template definitions), never guessed from a name. Each mapping records its source and the date checked. The admin screen exists only for later changes. A field nothing can fill is raised as a developer request | Owner | 2026-11-07 | Owner: "the default mapping you take care of it but not on a whim properly check the web … and only then map it". Was: the system suggests, an admin confirms each | decided |
| Q24 | A fixed value set for every product of a type: can the shop change it? | Yes, it is a default pre-filled on every product of the type; the shop can override it per product | Owner | 2026-11-07 | Owner's answer | decided |
| Q25 | Existing answers that don't fit a new question? | Keep both, never guess: the old answer stays as its own attribute, the new question starts empty | Owner | 2026-11-07 | Owner's answer; marketplace-integration Q42 (nothing filled from a guess) | decided |
| Q26 | Which new attributes are added now? | All four groups: bags (style, inner material, pockets, capacity, strap type as channels mean it), clothing (fabric type, care, size system and class), fragrance and incense (item form, unit count), jewellery (stone and metal details); plus generic name, model name and number of items for every type | Owner | 2026-11-07 | Owner ticked every group and the shared fields | decided |
| Q27 | How is the form laid out? | "Required now" (BB and connected channels), then "Also helps on…" (other channels) collapsed; each question shows small channel badges | Owner | 2026-11-07 | Owner's answer | decided |
| Q28 | A newly connected channel and existing products? | Home shows "38 products need 4 details for Flipkart" opening the bulk-fill page; a product is created on the channel only once complete | Owner | 2026-11-07 | Owner's answer | decided |
| Q29 | What must a brand have to be saved? | Name only; maker, packer and importer (with address and pincode) optional, completeness flags what each channel needs | Owner | 2026-11-07 | Owner's answer | decided |
| Q30 | When is the old model removed? | Right after dynamic-form, in the same release | Owner | 2026-11-07 | Owner's answer. Was recommended: two weeks after, with the nightly comparison clean | decided |
| Q31 | How do the subfeatures ship? | One release at the end: all five land together | Owner | 2026-11-07 | Owner's answer. Was recommended: each phase deploys separately | decided |
| Q32 | Which channels does the programme cover? | All five: Amazon, Flipkart and Meesho (marketplaces) and Google Shopping and Meta (feed channels). Google and Meta get the same catalogue, maps and completeness; their output is the shopfront feed, not an API create | Owner | 2026-11-07 | Owner: "make sure we also cover the meta and google channels as well". Today they have hand-typed maps read from Google's spec on 2026-10-04 | decided |
| Q33 | With one release and no waiting window, how is the data move kept safe? | The release runs the backfill and a full old-vs-new comparison of every product's values before the switch; any mismatch stops the release. The old tables are dropped by a separate migration in the same deploy only after the comparison is clean, and a database backup is taken first | Claude · reversible | 2026-11-07 | Q30 and Q31 remove the two-week window; the safety moves to a verified switch | decided |
| Q34 | Are UX prototypes built for this programme? | No. Each subfeature with screens keeps a `ux.md` (journeys, state matrix, copy), which the owner signs off in place of a prototype | Owner | 2026-11-07 | Owner: "we can skip prototype for this one … keep the ux document for reference". The mapping screen is read-and-edit (Q23) and the form's states are fully specified in text | decided |
