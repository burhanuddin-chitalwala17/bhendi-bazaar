# Decisions — own-store/storefront-themes

- **Verified:** 2026-11-01

> Replace a Q in place until it is implemented (end its why with "was: …"); supersede only implemented ones (ADR-0062).
> Owner decides money, policy, irreversible data, external commitments, taste.
> Claude decides the rest, marked `Claude · reversible`. Other documents cite Q ids. The
> parent's decisions bind here and are not repeated: own-store Q15 (three palettes, no free
> colour), Q22 (theme's own header and footer with platform marks; shared cart; platform
> checkout), Q23 ("Visit {Shop name}", "Visit shop" when long).
