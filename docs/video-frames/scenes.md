# Bhendi Bazaar brand films: scenes

Two 120-second films, each in 16:9 (1920×1080) and 9:16 (1080×1920). The frames in this folder are from the 16:9 versions, one per scene, taken mid-scene.

There is no voice-over. All messaging is on-screen text over a synthesised drum-led score (108 BPM, modern kit plus dhol and tabla). Each cut is marked by a saffron light sweep.

## Partners (`partners/`)

**In plain words**

- **Who a partner is:** a brand that sponsors or advertises with Bhendi Bazaar, shown in the film as a "YOUR BRAND" placeholder.
- **What they get:** the film shows three placements.
  1. A **home page banner** on bhendi-bazaar.com, labelled "Featured partner".
  2. A **category page** banner, shown on the Ridas category as "YOUR BRAND Presents Ridas".
  3. A place in **every marketing campaign** Bhendi Bazaar runs: co-branded posts, reels, stories and creatives, labelled "In partnership with YOUR BRAND" and "Presented by YOUR BRAND", plus a "Bhendi Bazaar × YOUR BRAND" lockup.

  The film also pitches the benefit: being seen as modern and supportive of small sellers, and being in front of the buyers who arrive from Google, Meta, Instagram, Amazon, Flipkart and WhatsApp.
- **Price or package:** none. The partner film mentions no price, package or traffic numbers.
- **What the partner is asked to do:** "Become a partner" (button), followed by "Talk to us · bhendi-bazaar.com/business".
