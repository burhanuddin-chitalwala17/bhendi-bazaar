# CLAUDE.md — channels domain

- **Verified:** 2026-11-25
- **Covers:** `server/channels/**`

> **Read the project-wide rules first:** [`/CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md). This file covers **channels** only. Requirements are in [docs/specs/marketplace-integration/](../../docs/specs/marketplace-integration/); every decision, with who made it, is in each spec's `decisions.md`.

## Purpose
Channels owns a shop's authorised connection to a marketplace, and to its own social accounts on Meta (Instagram, WhatsApp; ADR-0068, social-selling): the consent round trip, the encrypted tokens, their renewal, and a daily health check. Later subfeatures of marketplace-integration (import, merge, listings, stock sync, publish) act on a marketplace through this domain and never hold a credential themselves.

## Boundaries

**Owns:** `server/channels/**`, the `ChannelConnection` and `ChannelListing` tables (a read-only mirror of each marketplace listing, plus its link to a product), `PlatformListing` (each product's listing per marketplace as the shop means it: only its own values, what was last sent, and its publish), and stock sync's `ChannelLocation`, `MarketplaceOrder` and `StockCorrection`; for social channels, `MetaEvent` (each webhook change, stored once), `MetaUnroutedCount` (what the intake dropped, counted), `ConnectionLane` (one worker per account and lane) and `DataDeletionRequest` (a person's deletion request through Meta, naming nobody).

**Does not own:** where a shop says it sells (`listing`'s `OrgChannel`, written through `orgChannelService.setIn` in the same transaction as a connect or disconnect), the channel vocabulary (`server/shared/channels.ts`), or the encryption itself (`server/shared/crypto.ts`), or what becomes of a marketplace's refusal: errors are handed to `requests` as the shared `requests.listing-complaints` job, never by importing it (setup-requests D5).

## Rules

- **A token never leaves this domain.** The public surface is `channel-connection.service`, `channel-import.service`, `channel-settle.service`, `channel-facts.service`, `channel-family.service`, `channel-location.service`, `channel-stock.service`, `channel-orders.service`, `platform-listing.service`, `listing-publish.service`, `listing-create.service`, `listing-problems.service`, `channel-types.service` and their `.types`; they answer cards, listings, settling and outcomes. A later subfeature adds a verb to `MarketplaceProvider` and calls it here, through `accessToken()`, rather than asking for a token.
- **Every marketplace goes behind `MarketplaceProvider`.** No marketplace-specific branching outside `providers/<name>/`. A fact about one marketplace's app (Amazon's unlisted cap and `version=beta`) lives in its provider, not in a flag.
- **Renewal is serialised by a row lock.** Anything that renews or re-seals tokens takes `lockSecrets` inside a transaction and writes both tokens and expiries in one update. Flipkart replaces its refresh token near expiry; a second, unlocked renewal would keep the old one and disconnect the shop silently.
- **Marketplace text never reaches a person or a log**, save the one exception below. Failures become `MarketplaceAuthError` (the seller must reconnect) or `MarketplaceUnavailableError` (try later), and outcomes are `CONNECT_RESULT` / `CONNECTION_ERROR` codes the UI maps to ux.md's copy. Response bodies are never logged: a token can appear in them.
- **One switch.** `CHANNELS_CONNECT` (`channel-availability.ts`) lists the marketplaces open to connect, `SOCIAL_CONNECT` the social platforms, and neither is read anywhere else.
- **Social channels have their own lock**, Social selling (`socialPlan` in `channel-plan.ts`; social-selling A-D21): every Instagram and WhatsApp action asserts it, as marketplace actions assert Channels. Instagram and WhatsApp are `SOCIAL_PLATFORM`s, never members of `CHANNEL` (ADR-0068 decision 3; connect-instagram Q14).
- **Channels is a paid feature** (subscriptions/feature-access D5, D8). Every shop-facing action asserts it through `channel-plan.ts`, the only file here that reaches `subscriptions`; `accessToken` asserts it too, so a marketplace call added later cannot act for a shop without it. A pass over many shops reads the holders once; a per-shop job for a shop without it ends succeeded with a skip. Reads that draw a screen, disconnect, pausing sync and token renewal stay free.
- **A settle links every listing or none.** The link is conditional inside the transaction that makes or updates the product; a short count rolls it back. Photo addresses are re-read from the marketplace on the server, never taken from a request.
- **A read never deletes a listing and never undoes the owner.** Rows are keyed on (connection, SKU) so a retried read writes the same ones; only a finished read marks the unseen gone; a link is written only to a row with no product and no owner's unlink.
- **A fill never overwrites.** Filling from a listing writes through catalog's and listing's conditional commands, which lock the row and write only what is still empty; the card is computed from the stored read, never stored as questions (marketplace-facts D6, D7).
- **Stock sync sends what is true when it runs.** A push reads the product's counts under `pg_advisory_xact_lock` and skips counts already sent; never send a delta. An order line is claimed once (unique on connection, order, line) and a restock is claimed by a conditional update. Nothing runs on a short schedule: work that repeats re-enqueues itself only while there is something to do.
- **A listing is computed, never stored whole.** What a marketplace gets is the product with the `PlatformListing`'s own values over it; the mirror is never repurposed as intent. A save compares on `version`; a publish sends only the reviewed fields at the reviewed version; its claimed state is the lock, so the marketplace is called outside any transaction and the answer is written only while the listing is still Sent. Only what the marketplace took becomes sent (Amazon's after a look confirms it), and every send and look ends in a recorded state.
- **A listing keeps photos by address, never by media id**: a product save renews its media rows. What a marketplace fetches is made at the send: a format it refuses (our WebP, for Amazon) goes as a `marketplaceJpeg` rendition.
- **A pass over every shop reads once and writes once.** Its marketplace calls are per account (each has its own token and limit); its statements are not per shop: one read of what it needs, one write of every answer (marketplace-publish Q22, Q23; inventory-sync Q26).
- **A marketplace's issue message may reach the shop** (marketplace-publish Q6): the issue's own sentence, plain text, cut to 300 characters. Never a response body.
- **Never call a live marketplace from a test.** Stub `fetch`.
- **Every Meta call goes through the one Graph client** (`providers/meta/graph-client.ts`; social-selling TRD A-D4): the pinned Graph version, `appsecret_proof`, a ten-second timeout, the answer parsed with the caller's schema, failures as `MetaError` carrying our `SOCIAL_ERROR` code (`social/types.ts`), one log line with no body, token or Meta text. A call that changes something passes `mayHaveTakenEffect`, so a timeout is `UNCERTAIN`, never retried blind (ADR-0086). Meta's published limits live in `meta-limits.ts` with their source and date; the app's settings are read only in `providers/meta/settings.ts`.
- **Meta's webhooks are stored, never acted on, in the request** (ADR-0084): `meta-intake.service` verifies the signature (current secret, or the previous one while rotating), splits the delivery with `providers/meta/webhook-split.ts`, writes each change once by its dedupe key with its payload sealed, and counts what it drops. An event for an account no shop holds keeps no payload and answers 200.
- **Meta's account callbacks are signed requests** (meta-foundation D6, D7): `meta-callbacks.service` verifies them with `providers/meta/signed-request.ts` under the product's secret. A removal (Instagram's deauthorize, or WhatsApp's `removed`/`offboarded` account update through `channels.sinks.ts`) only turns the card to Needs reconnecting, and only when the notice is newer than the connection's last connect (`markRemoved`). A deletion request stores the person's id as a keyed hash and sealed, never plain; `channels.delete-person` runs every `Forgetter` registered in `server/shared/social-events/forget.ts` and marks it done only after all succeed, blanking the sealed id; the page says done only once raw events have aged out too (meta-foundation Q7). The status page reads state and dates by reference, nothing else.
- **Raw events live 30 days** (meta-foundation Q2): `channels.meta-retention` deletes them in batches of 1,000 by `receivedAt`, and `channels.meta-sweep` re-asks what a lost job left; both are queued by `maintain`, never on a schedule slot (Q8).
- **Stored events are handed on by the drain, per account, in order** (ADR-0084): `meta-drain.service` takes the connection's `events` lease (`connection-lane.repository`, one holder per connection and lane), reads pending events by when they happened, translates them with `providers/meta/translate.ts` into the neutral facts of `server/shared/social-events/`, and hands each run of one kind to the sinks other domains register. The lease is renewed before every hand-over and nothing is written once it is lost. A failing sink stops the run so order holds: a sink that was down only delays the event, backing off from 30 seconds to 15 minutes; an event that cannot be read, or that a sink refuses with `PermanentFactError`, dies on its fifth try and the run moves on. A drain request whose queue slot is taken uses a spare slot, never silently dropped. Meta's vocabulary (billing categories, template states, account events) becomes closed sets in `translate.ts` and nowhere else. This domain imports no sink.

## Structure

| Path | Holds |
|---|---|
| `channel-connection.service.ts` | The public surface: overview cards, connect, disconnect, check, the daily `maintain` pass |
| `channel-connection.types.ts` | Closed sets and DTOs |
| `channel-connection.repository.ts`, `channel-connection-passes.repository.ts` | The one writer of `ChannelConnection`; read-only projections for passes over many shops and for routing Meta's events |
| `connect-state.ts` | The signed OAuth `state` |
| `channel-availability.ts` | The one switch: `CHANNELS_CONNECT` for marketplaces, `SOCIAL_CONNECT` for Instagram and WhatsApp |
| `channel-plan.ts` | Whether a shop holds Channels: the assert every action makes, and the holders among many for a pass |
| `channel-import.service.ts` | The listing read the worker runs, Unlink, Link back, the Listings page and a product's links (channel-import) |
| `channel-listing.repository.ts` | The one writer of `ChannelListing` |
| `listing-match.ts` | Which listing links to which product by SKU, how similar two names are, and which unsettled listings look like one item; pure |
| `channel-settle.service.ts` | Settling a listing: the Settle page's live read, adding or matching in one transaction, the photo copy (product-merge) |
| `channel-facts.service.ts`, `channel-facts.types.ts` | Filling a product from its linked listings: the job's read and fill, the product page's cards and their commands, the Settle page's type question (marketplace-facts) |
| `channel-family.service.ts`, `channel-family.types.ts` | One family from several listings: the family page's live read and plan, and the save that makes the family and every member or nothing (family-merge) |
| `family-plan.ts` | A family's axes, each member's values, what all share, and what stops a save; pure |
| `channel-location.service.ts`, `channel-location.repository.ts` | A marketplace's pickup locations, read on connect and Read again, and the shop's match for each (inventory-sync D1) |
| `channel-stock.service.ts`, `channel-stock.types.ts`, `stock-plan.ts` | Sending our counts per matched location under a lock per product, starting sync once locations are matched (inventory-sync Q28), Home's stock line, the daily check; `stock-plan.ts` is pure |
| `channel-orders.service.ts`, `marketplace-order.repository.ts` | The ten-minute order check, batched across shops (inventory-sync Q26): each line once, stock taken or the line flagged oversold, a cancellation restocked once; the Marketplace orders tab |
| `server/shared/jobs/stock-changed.job.ts` (shared), `channelStockService.pushChanged` | A stock change, one job per transaction from catalog, kept to the products a marketplace with sync on lists before a push is queued for each (inventory-sync Q27) |
| `stock-checks.job.ts` | The order check's and daily check's job specs: the order check enqueues its next run only while a connection has sync on (inventory-sync Q23) |
| `stock-correction.repository.ts` | The one writer of `StockCorrection`, the daily check's record |
| `fact-fill.ts` | Which type a listing is and which facts it fills or asks; pure, the same for every marketplace |
| `listing-facts.job.ts` | The fill job's spec, enqueued by a settle; its handler is in `channels.jobs.ts` |
| `listing-photos.ts`, `listing-photos.job.ts` | Which photo addresses a copy may fetch; the copy job's spec |
| `listing-read.job.ts` | The read job's spec, enqueued on connect and by Read again; its handler is in `channels.jobs.ts` |
| `platform-listing.service.ts`, `platform-listing.repository.ts`, `platform-listing.types.ts`, `listing-plan.ts` | Each product's listing per marketplace: made with what is live, computed from the product, saved as its own values; `listing-plan.ts` is pure (channel-listings) |
| `listing-publish.service.ts`, `listing-publish.job.ts` | The review, the publish claimed at the reviewed version, the send under a lock per listing, and the answer check, run when the next look falls due and batched across shops (marketplace-publish D3–D5, Q22) |
| `listing-problems.service.ts`, `listing-problems.job.ts` | The daily problems look for every shop in one run: one read of every account with its token, one marketplace call per account, one write of every answer; Home's line (marketplace-publish Q23) |
| `listing-photos-out.ts` | The photo addresses a marketplace fetches: as stored where it takes the format, else a JPEG rendition (marketplace-publish D2) |
| `listing-create.service.ts`, `listing-create.ts` | Putting a product or a family on a marketplace: looking for its page, the draft, the check-only answer, the create; `listing-create.ts` (theme, SKU, the compliance lines from the brand's parties) is pure (marketplace-publish D6–D8; listing-model attributes Q11) |
| `generic-brand.ts` | Whether a product lists on Amazon as Generic: its brand's choice, else the shop's default brand's (listing-model Q10, dynamic-form Q14) |
| `channel-catalogue.service.ts` | Reads each marketplace's own types into listing's channel catalogue, through the home brand, once a day from `maintain`; a marketplace with no definitions reads nothing (listing-model/channel-catalogue D2) |
| `channel-types.service.ts` | A marketplace's type definitions and type search, read through one org's connection (the home brand's) for requests; the token stays here (setup-requests D7, D10) |
| `listing-complaints.ts` | Hands a send's, create's or look's errors to requests as one job per batch, in the caller's transaction; a failed hand-over is logged, never thrown (setup-requests D5, ADR-0053) |
| `meta-intake.service.ts`, `meta-event.repository.ts`, `meta-unrouted.repository.ts`, `meta-event.types.ts` | The webhook intake and the one writers of `MetaEvent` and `MetaUnroutedCount` (meta-foundation D3) |
| `meta-drain.service.ts`, `meta-drain.job.ts`, `connection-lane.repository.ts` | The drain and its job spec (handler in `channels.jobs.ts`); the one writer of `ConnectionLane` and the `LANE` set (meta-foundation D4, D5) |
| `meta-callbacks.service.ts`, `data-deletion.repository.ts`, `data-deletion.job.ts`, `channels.sinks.ts` | Deauthorize and data deletion, the one writer of `DataDeletionRequest`, the forget job's spec (handler in `channels.jobs.ts`); channels' own sinks: connection health from account updates (meta-foundation D6, D7), and message access from any message in (connect-instagram D3) |
| `social-owners.service.ts`, `social-owners.repository.ts` | The shop behind each social connection and its own account ids, for other domains' sinks (inbox-core D2); a read, no token |
| `meta-upkeep.service.ts`, `meta-upkeep.job.ts` | The daily upkeep, queued by `maintain`: raw events past 30 days deleted in batches, drains and deletion requests a lost job left re-asked (meta-foundation D10, Q8) |
| `instagram-connect.service.ts` | Connecting Instagram (connect-instagram D1): the consent link, and the return, which stores the 60-day token sealed for a business or creator account no other shop holds, under the row lock, then asks for webhooks |
| `whatsapp-connect.service.ts`, `whatsapp-setup.job.ts` | Connecting WhatsApp through Meta's Embedded Signup (connect-whatsapp D1, D2): the attempt the pop-up opens with (its nonce kept on the shop's row, so a result is spent once), and the result, confirmed with Meta, claiming the number Setting up under the row lock and queueing `channels.whatsapp-setup` |
| `instagram-connection.service.ts` | Keeping Instagram connected and ending it (connect-instagram D2, D4, D5): its own daily pass from `maintain` (the 60-day token renewed under `lockSecrets` once older than 7 days, the account read, webhooks asked for again, re-sealing), Check now, the card's message-access facts (the sheet's Done, Meta's refusal), and disconnect |
| `whatsapp-catalogue/item.ts` | A product as its WhatsApp catalogue item, or left out with why, and the item's content hash; pure (whatsapp-catalogue D1, D2) |
| `social/types.ts` | Our words for the social platforms: `SOCIAL_PLATFORM`, its labels, `SOCIAL_ERROR` (social-selling TRD A-D3) |
| `social/checklist.ts` | A social card's setup checklist, computed from facts on each read and never stored; pure (connect-instagram D4) |
| `providers/meta/` | The Meta platform layer: `graph-client.ts` (the one client), `errors.ts` (Meta's codes in ours), `meta-limits.ts` (dated limits and lane pace), `settings.ts` (the app's settings, the secret under rotation), `signature.ts` (Meta's signature and verify token in constant time), `signed-request.ts` (the callbacks' signed request), `instagram-login.ts` (Instagram Login: consent, token exchange and refresh, the account read, the webhook subscription), `embedded-signup.ts` (WhatsApp Embedded Signup: the code exchanged for the business token, the pop-up's ids confirmed), `webhook-split.ts` (a delivery into items and their dedupe keys; pure), `translate.ts` (a stored event into facts; pure) |
| `providers/` | `MarketplaceProvider`, one folder per marketplace (`*.listings.ts` reads its listings, `*.stock.ts` its locations, counts and orders, `*.publish.ts` its listing rules, publish, status and create; `stock.ts` and `publish.ts` the shared shapes), and `listing-read.ts`'s shared sink, retry and paise helpers |
